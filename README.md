## Game Pitch

**Wendogo** is a multiplayer **social deduction card game** where players take on the roles of **survivors**… and a **Wendogo hidden among them**.

Each player knows their own role, but the identities of the others remain secret.  
By day, players must **cooperate** to survive: gathering resources, managing supplies, and preparing for the worst.  
But when night falls, masks may slip — and the monster may strike.

The survivors must complete a **ritual** to banish the creature before the end of **12 turns** (6 days and 6 nights), or kill the Wendogo directly.  
The Wendogo wins by **eliminating all survivors** or simply **surviving until the end of the 12th turn**.

<br>

## Core Gameplay Loop

Wendogo is built around **hidden roles, bluffing, cooperation, and betrayal**, inspired by modern board games.

### Day Phase
- Players openly discuss, negotiate, and plan strategies  
- Resources are gathered and shared  
- Progress toward the ritual is discussed  
- Players must manage food and survival  

At the **start of each day**, every player must consume **1 unit of food**.  
If a player cannot do so, they **automatically lose 1 health point**, increasing pressure on both individual and group resource management.

<br>

### Night Phase
- Players act **one by one**
- All actions are **hidden**
- No immediate feedback is given  

Only at dawn do the consequences become visible:
- Resource gains or losses  
- Damage dealt  
- Ritual progression  
- Votes and suspicions  

This delayed resolution creates **tactical uncertainty**, fueling paranoia, doubt, and mistrust among players.

<br>

## Win Conditions

### Survivors Victory
Survivors win by completing the **ritual**:

- The ritual card requires **X resources of each type**
- Resources must be spent collectively
- Once the required amount is reached, the **Wendogo is banished**

Alternatively, the survivors can win by **killing the Wendogo**.

<br>

### Wendogo Victory
The Wendogo wins if:
- All survivors are eliminated  
- OR the Wendogo survives until the **end of the 12th turn** without the ritual being completed  

<br>

## Voice Chat & Social Interaction

Wendogo features **real-time voice chat** during matches.

Players can:
- Share information (or lies)
- Negotiate alliances
- Coordinate strategies
- Accuse, defend, and manipulate others  

For example, players may debate whether to:
- Focus on completing the ritual  
- Or hunt down the Wendogo based on the game’s current state  

Voice communication is a **core pillar** of the experience, reinforcing immersion, tension, and social dynamics.
